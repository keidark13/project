Trust + calm + a booking flow that respects time.

Dental owners pay premium for booking funnels because patient LTV runs $5K-$20K. The three variants below cover the spectrum: family-spa for general practice, tech-modern for boutique implant/cosmetic clinics, and editorial-magazine for aesthetic-only studios.

02 — Conversion architecture

The Gus Sins 10-Point Sales Page applied.

Same order across all three variants

How each variant carries the structure

Every variant ships in the same 12-section order. Only the design vocabulary changes per niche and per direction.

  1. Big PromiseHero outcome headline with italic accent on transformation noun
  2. Empathy / Pain3-card grid mirroring patient struggle in their own language
  3. Opportunity / MechanismWhy this practice was built differently (spa-care / Halo-Method / studio-not-clinic)
  4. Before vs AfterSensory contrast: old dental visit vs visiting this practice
  5. USPSingle distinguishing sentence about cap / mechanism / philosophy
  6. Offer Stack4-item welcome visit deliverable with $ values and one-price summary
  7. Social ProofMagazine asymmetric grid with metric chips + Unsplash face-crop avatars
  8. Risk ReversalSame-day reschedule promise OR 30-day fit redo OR 10-year aesthetic warranty
  9. AuthorityDelta/Aetna/MetLife insurance + ADA + practice years badge
  10. Urgency + FAQ + CloseReal cap (8 slots/month) + 6-item FAQ accordion + closing CTA
