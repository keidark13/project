FAQ

The questions every founder asks on the first call.

Is this group coaching or 1:1?
1:1 only. No cohort. No group calls. No Slack community. The peer-bench introductions are direct intros, not a shared room.
What revenue range does this work for?
$2M to roughly $20M ARR. Below $2M your bottleneck is usually offer or distribution. Above $20M you need an operator hire, not a coach.
How much of my time does this take per week?
Two 90-minute calls per month. Voxer is asynchronous. Most clients spend 3-5 hours per week on the work itself, including implementation with the team.
Do you guarantee a revenue outcome?
No. Anyone who guarantees a revenue number in coaching is selling something else. I guarantee the work and the 30-day exit if it's not a fit.
What if I'm not actually stuck, just want to scale faster?
Then this is probably the wrong engagement. The Operator Diagnostic is built for founders hitting a real ceiling. If the business already compounds, your bottleneck is elsewhere.
