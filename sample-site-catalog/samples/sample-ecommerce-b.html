Why Wynnfield

Three principles, eighty products, zero shortcuts.

Designed to last decades

Every Wynnfield piece is engineered for daily use across generations. Solid brass, cast iron, kiln-fired stoneware, heavyweight linen — materials that age into character rather than failing into landfill.

Small-batch crafted

We work with eleven independent makers across Oregon, Ohio, and Belgium. No factory floors, no mass-runs. Each batch is fewer than 200 pieces — which means we sell out, and that is the point.

Repairable, not disposable

Wear it out. Send it back. We will repair or replace any Wynnfield product for five years from purchase — including parts, labor, and return shipping. The most sustainable product is the one you do not throw away.

Questions

Frequently asked

Operational details about shipping, returns, repair, and how we work with retailers. Email hello@wynnfieldco.example for anything else.

How fast do you ship and where do you ship from?
Orders ship from our Portland, Oregon workshop within 2 business days. Standard US delivery takes 3-5 business days via USPS or UPS Ground. Express 2-day and next-day options are available at checkout. You will receive a tracking link by email within four hours of dispatch.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes. We ship to 38 countries via DHL Express, including Canada, the UK, EU member states, Australia, Japan, and Singapore. International orders typically arrive in 5-10 business days. Duties and taxes are calculated and charged at checkout — no surprise customs bills on delivery.
What is your return policy?
30-day returns on any unused product in original condition, no questions asked. We pay return shipping for US orders — a prepaid label arrives by email within 24 hours of your request. International returns are at customer cost. Refunds process within 3 business days of arrival.
Do you offer gift wrap?
Yes. Recycled kraft gift wrap with a hand-tied jute ribbon is $6 per item. You can add a written note (up to 240 characters) at no extra cost. Select the gift-wrap option on the cart page — we will omit invoice details and pricing from the shipment.
What does the 5-year repair guarantee cover?
Any manufacturing defect or normal-wear failure within 5 years of purchase. We repair if possible — re-seasoning, re-finishing, hardware replacement, glaze touch-up. We replace with new stock if the piece is unrepairable. You ship to us, we cover the repair work and the return shipping. Damage from misuse is not covered, but we will quote a paid repair.
What is B-stock and how does it work?
B-stock is product with minor cosmetic flaws — uneven glaze on stoneware, finish marks on brass, weave irregularities on linen — that do not affect function or longevity. We sell these at 25-40% off in the Sale section. Quantities are extremely limited (often a single unit) and B-stock still carries the full 5-year repair guarantee.
Do you offer wholesale pricing?
Yes. We work with 64 independent retailers and design studios across North America, the UK, and Japan. Minimum first order is $500 at standard wholesale margin (keystone, 50% off retail). Reorders have no minimum. Apply through the wholesale form linked in the footer — we approve applications within 5 business days.
How is Wynnfield sustainable?
Four operational commitments: (1) Plastic-free packaging — recycled kraft, jute twine, shredded paper void fill, no plastic anywhere in the shipment. (2) Carbon-neutral shipping via Pacific Forest Trust offset partnership, certified annually. (3) The 5-year repair guarantee, which keeps roughly 1,200 pieces a year out of landfill. (4) Small-batch production capped at 200 units, which prevents the dead-inventory overstock that drives most homewares waste. The most sustainable product is the one you do not throw away.
