03 · The problem

Why founders give up on naming.

Three reasons your brand has been a Google Doc with one ugly logo for six months. We watched 400 of them happen before we built this.

Naming paralysis

You have 80 names in a doc. None feel right. The domains are gone. Every time you pick one, you read it back the next morning and want to throw your laptop in the river.

Brand designer wait times

Good independent designers are booked six to eight weeks out. Quotes start at four thousand dollars and that is for the logo alone. Meanwhile your launch slipped a quarter.

Hodge-podge brand kit

A free Canva logo. A palette from a colors-dot-com tab. A font from a Twitter screenshot. Nothing matches. Every new asset looks like a different company shipped it.

11 · Questions

Things people ask before buying.

If yours isn’t here, email hello@studiofield.example and a real human replies in under four hours.

Can I use Studio Field kits commercially?
Yes. Every kit on every plan — including the free Solo plan — includes a commercial-use license. Use it for your startup, client work, products, ads, anywhere. No royalties, no attribution required, no expiry.
Do you run a trademark check?
We screen names against USPTO and EU IPO live trademark databases, plus the top fifteen country registries and major domain registrars, before we show them to you. Conflicts get flagged in the kit. For final clearance — especially for premium or international launches — we still recommend a real IP lawyer.
What file formats do I get?
Logo: SVG, PNG (transparent), PDF. Palette: HEX, RGB, HSL, plus a Figma Tokens file and a copy-paste Tailwind config block. Type pair: Google Fonts references with a CSS @import line and a system-font fallback stack. Social kit: 12 PNGs sized for Instagram (1:1, 4:5, 9:16), X (16:9), and LinkedIn. Pro and Team also get the editable Figma source file.
Can I edit the kit after I generate it?
Yes. Every output opens in our in-browser editor. Swap colors, regenerate the logo mark, switch type pairs, tweak the social kit, re-roll any individual piece. Re-export any time. Your kits live in your account forever, even if you downgrade.
Can I request a specific style or vibe?
Yes. The prompt accepts style notes like “serious financial”, “playful but premium”, “Tokyo street”, “1970s NYC”, “Y2K but tasteful”. On Pro and Team plans you can also upload a moodboard of up to ten reference images and Studio Field will respect the visual direction.
How is this different from Midjourney or DALL·E?
Those tools generate one image at a time. Studio Field generates a system — logo, palette, type, voice, and templated assets that all coordinate. The logo comes out as an editable vector, not a flat PNG. The palette is internally consistent and accessibility-checked. The social kit reuses the same elements so everything looks like one company shipped it.
Who owns the brand kit?
You do. Full assignment. We do not retain rights to your generated kit. We do not train future models on your prompts or your outputs — your inputs are private to your account and deleted on request. Studio Field is SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliant.
What if I hate the kit?
Two safety nets. One: regenerate as many times as you want — every prompt produces a new direction, and you can also tweak the prompt to nudge things. Two: if you upgrade to Pro or Team and still aren’t happy within 14 days, email us for a full refund. You keep the files. We’d rather you walked away whole.
