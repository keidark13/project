04 — The method How Maison Derm works

The natural-results-first philosophy — in four moves we don't skip.

The protocol Dr. Park spent her dermatology residency arguing for. It's why we cap the day at eight, not twenty-two.

Fig. 01 · Skin assessment, treatment room two

  1. 01 /

    Diagnose the face, not the line

    3D imaging + dermatology assessment first. We map what your face is doing. Half of "I hate my forehead lines" is really a conversation about brow position.

  2. 02 /

    Plan in seasons, not appointments

    You leave the consult with a six-month written roadmap: what we treat, what we wait on, what we never touch. Line-item cost before anything books.

  3. 03 /

    Cap the day at eight

    Most injectors run eighteen to twenty-five. We run eight. That's how Dr. Park spends forty minutes mixing a custom filler dilution — instead of suggesting "while you're here."

  4. 04 /

    Photograph everything, quarterly

    Same lighting. Same crop. Same camera. You see what's working, what isn't, and what the next ninety days should do. We bring receipts, not a sales pitch.

11 — Promises The fine print

In your favour.

The promise we sign at consult. And the questions we get most.

M

The Refreshed-Not-Overdone Promise

If, within fourteen days of any treatment, you feel the result doesn't match the plan we agreed to in writing, we correct or dissolve at no charge. Tox and filler correction included.

In the plan you sign · not in a footer

I've had bad work before. Can Maison fix it?

Often, yes — hyaluronidase to dissolve overdone filler, time to let tox wear off, or a wait-and-watch plan. We won't promise anything we can't deliver. The first consult tells you exactly what's possible.

Is Maison only for women?

No. About 28% of Maison members are men — mostly 35-55, mostly tox and medical-grade skincare. Dr. Park trained on male facial anatomy specifically.

What does this actually cost in a typical year?

Membership is $89/mo. Members spend an average of $2,400-$3,800/yr in treatments at member pricing. Your line-item year sits in your inbox before you book a thing.

Why cap the day at eight per injector?

Because rushed injection is the #1 reason results look "off." At eight, every appointment gets 60 minutes of real attention. Volume clinics see 18-25 because that's what their unit economics demand. We chose smaller on purpose.

Do you accept insurance?

Aesthetic treatments are not covered. We accept HSA/FSA for medical-grade skincare and certain medical-dermatology consults.

What if I need to cancel my consult?

No fee if cancelled at least 24 hrs ahead. Same-day no-shows are charged a $35 hold fee — this protects the eight-cap for everyone else on the schedule.
