The Ironclad promise

If we install it, we own it — for as long as you own the house.

Every install we touch carries a lifetime workmanship warranty. That means if anything goes sideways because of how we wired, mounted, or routed your system — we’re back out, no charge, no questions, no “well that was outside the scope” conversation.

Combine that with the manufacturer warranty on the equipment itself (10-12 years parts on Carrier/Trane/Daikin), and you’ve got a system that’s genuinely protected for the next decade-plus.

Common questions

Before you book, here’s what people ask.

How much is the diagnostic, and what does it cover?

$89 flat. The tech arrives within your 60-minute window, runs a full systems check (refrigerant levels, electrical, airflow, drainage), and gives you a written flat-rate repair quote before any work happens. If you approve the repair, the $89 is credited back — the diagnostic is effectively free.

Are you really available 24/7? Like, even Sunday at 2am?

Yes — a real human, not a voicemail. We staff our after-hours line with our own techs (rotating on-call), not a national call center. After-hours service has a $149 trip charge, also credited back if you book the repair.

Do you offer financing on new systems?

Yes. 0% APR for 18 months on installs over $4,000, through Wells Fargo or Synchrony. Approval in under 5 minutes from the kitchen table.

What if you don’t service the brand I have?

We service every major residential brand — including the discount brands we don’t install. If the system is salvageable, we’ll repair it. If it’s on its last legs, we’ll tell you straight (no upsell pressure).

Are your techs background-checked and drug-tested?

Every Ironclad tech passes a federal background check + 5-panel drug screen before their first ride-along, and is re-screened annually. We send you a photo + bio of the tech assigned before they arrive.
