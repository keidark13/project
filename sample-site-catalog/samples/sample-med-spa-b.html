How NORA works

The natural-results-first philosophy — four moves we don't skip.

This is what we argued for during residency and what we cap our day around. It's why we'll never become the volume clinic across town.

  1. 01

    Diagnose the face, not the line

    3D imaging + dermatology assessment first. We map what your face is actually doing. Half of "I hate my forehead lines" is really a brow-position conversation.

  2. 02

    Plan in seasons, not appointments

    You leave consult with a six-month written roadmap: what we treat, what we wait on, what we never touch. Line-item cost before anything books.

  3. 03

    Cap the day at eight

    Most injectors run 18-25 a day. We run 8. That's why we can spend forty minutes mixing a custom filler dilution instead of pushing you toward "while you're here."

  4. 04

    Photograph everything, quarterly

    Same lighting. Same crops. Same camera. You see what's working, what isn't, and what the next 90 days should do. We bring the receipts, not the sales pitch.

Promises & common questions

The fine print, in your favour.

N

The Refreshed-Not-Frozen Promise

If, within 14 days of any treatment, you feel the result doesn't match the plan we agreed to in writing, we correct or dissolve at no charge. Tox/filler correction included.

This sits in the plan you sign at consult. Not in a footer.

I've had bad work before. Can NORA fix it?

Often, yes — hyaluronidase to dissolve overdone filler, time to let tox wear off, or a wait-and-watch plan. We won't promise anything we can't deliver. Your first consult tells you exactly what's possible.

Is NORA only for women?

No. About 28% of NORA members are men — mostly 35-55, mostly tox + medical-grade skincare. Dr. Park trained on male facial anatomy specifically.

What does this actually cost in a typical year?

Membership is $89/mo. Members spend an average of $2,400-$3,800/yr in treatments at member pricing. Your line-item year sits in your inbox before you book anything.

Why cap each injector at eight per day?

Because rushed injection is the #1 reason results look "off." At eight, every appointment gets 60 minutes of actual attention. Volume clinics need 18-25 because that's what their unit economics demand. We chose smaller on purpose.

Do you accept insurance?

Aesthetic treatments are not covered. We accept HSA/FSA for medical-grade skincare and certain medical-dermatology consults.

What if I cancel my consult?

No fee if cancelled 24 hrs ahead. Same-day no-shows are charged a $35 hold fee — this protects the eight-cap for everyone else on the schedule.
