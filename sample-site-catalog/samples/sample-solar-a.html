How much does going solar actually cost?

It depends on your roof and usage, which is exactly what the free estimate measures. Many homeowners pay $0 down through financing and replace their utility bill with a lower fixed payment.

What happens on cloudy days or at night?

Your system stays connected to the grid, so you always have power. Add a battery and you store daytime production to use after dark or during outages.

Do I really get the tax credit?

If you owe federal tax and own the system, you typically qualify. We break down your exact eligibility in writing during the estimate, never a vague promise.

Will solar work on my roof?

Most roof types and orientations work. The satellite analysis tells us for sure before anyone climbs up there.

What if I sell my house?

Owned solar generally adds resale value and transfers with the home. We walk you through it so there are no surprises.
