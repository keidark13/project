Our approach

We built Linden around the spa-care model.

Dentistry doesn't have to be a conveyor belt. By slowing down, scoping every visit in advance, and treating your nervous system like part of the patient, we make care actually feel like care.

Pillar 01

Slow rooms, full hours

One patient per provider, per hour. No double-booking. You get the time you paid for, every single visit.

Pillar 02

Transparent before you arrive

Insurance pre-checked, treatment plan emailed in advance, every line item explained before a tool touches your teeth.

Pillar 03

Comfort as the default

Heated chairs, weighted blankets, noise-cancelling headphones, and a numbing protocol you approve. Anxiety is a real diagnosis here.

FAQ

Questions families ask before booking.

Do you accept my insurance? +

We are in-network with Delta Dental, Aetna, MetLife, Cigna and Guardian, and we file out-of-network claims for almost every other major carrier. Tell us your plan when you book and we will pre-verify your benefits before your visit.

What if I haven't seen a dentist in years? +

Then you are exactly who this practice was built for. There is no lecture, no shame, no judgment. We start with the Welcome Visit, build a 12-month plan around what you actually want to fix, and pace everything to your comfort and budget.

Is the $99 Welcome Visit really all-in? +

Yes. The exam, x-rays, cleaning, polish, and 12-month care plan are all included for $99 with no hidden fees. If we spot something during the visit that needs extra treatment, we will quote it before any work begins.

Can you handle anxious or fearful patients? +

About a third of our patients describe themselves as dental-anxious. We offer nitrous, oral conscious and IV sedation, plus comfort tools like noise-cancelling headphones and weighted blankets. Tell us what scares you and we will plan around it.

Do you see kids? +

From age 2 up. We use a gentle first-visit script, let kids ride the chair, and never restrain a child. If your little one is not ready, we will book a short, no-treatment "meet and greet" first.

How far in advance do I need to book? +

For routine new-patient visits we are usually booking 5 to 10 days out. For dental emergencies, call before noon and we will see you the same afternoon.
