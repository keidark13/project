Why we work this way

The natural-results-first philosophy — explained in four moves.

This is the protocol Dr. Park spent her dermatology residency arguing for. It's why we cap the day at 8, not 22.

  1. 01

    Diagnose the face, not the line

    3D imaging + dermatology assessment first. We map what your face is doing, not just what you don't like. Half of "I hate my forehead lines" is actually a brow-position story.

  2. 02

    Plan in seasons, not appointments

    You leave consult with a six-month written plan: what we treat, what we wait on, what we never touch. You see the cost line by line before anyone books a follow-up.

  3. 03

    Cap the day at 8 per injector

    Volume injectors do 18-25 clients a day. We cap at 8. That's why Dr. Park can spend forty minutes mixing a custom filler dilution instead of running to the next room.

  4. 04

    Photograph everything, quarterly

    Every 90 days we re-shoot under the same lighting. You see what's working, what isn't, and what the plan should do next. No selling. Just receipts.

Promises & common questions

The fine print, in your favour.

M

The Refreshed-Not-Frozen Promise

If, within 14 days of any treatment, you feel your result doesn't match the plan we agreed to in writing, we'll correct or dissolve at no charge. Tox/filler correction included.

This is in the plan you sign at consult. Not buried in a footer.

I've had bad work before. Can you fix it?

Sometimes, yes — with hyaluronidase to dissolve overdone filler, time to let tox wear off, or a wait-and-watch plan. We don't promise anything we can't deliver. The first consult tells you exactly what's possible.

Are you only for women?

No. About 28% of Maven members are men — mostly 35-55, mostly tox + medical-grade skincare. Dr. Park trained on male anatomy specifically; the plan is built for your face, not a category.

What does this actually cost in a typical year?

Membership is $89/mo. Members spend an average of $2,400-$3,800/yr in treatments (member pricing). The plan you get at consult shows your line-item year before you book anything.

Why cap each injector at 8 clients a day?

Because rushed injection is the #1 reason results look "off." At 8 clients, every appointment has 60 minutes of real attention. Volume clinics see 18-25 because that's what the unit economics demand. We chose smaller on purpose.

Do you take insurance?

Aesthetic treatments aren't covered by insurance. We accept HSA/FSA for medical-grade skincare and certain medical-dermatology consults.

What if I cancel my consult?

No fee if cancelled at least 24 hrs ahead. Same-day no-shows are charged a $35 hold fee — this is what protects the 8-cap for everyone else.
