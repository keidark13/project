The Honest Part

Moving day shouldn't ruin the week before and the week after.

Most moving stories don't start at 8 AM on move day. They start at the third quote that doesn't match the first, and end with a couch leg the wrong shape on a Tuesday in a new kitchen.

01 / The Quote

The number on the phone is never the number on the invoice.

You get an estimate. Three guys show up. Suddenly it's "the stairs," "the wrapping," "the long carry." The check is four hundred dollars heavier than expected and the dispatcher is on lunch.

02 / The Crew

You don't actually know who's about to walk through your front door.

Day labor, contractors, whoever the foreman called that morning. Nobody on the truck has been with the company longer than the new dishwasher in the kitchen they're packing.

03 / The Aftermath

Something breaks. The claim form is twenty-two pages long.

Your dresser has a fresh gouge. You call. You get voicemail. You email. You get a PDF that requires notarization and "documented purchase price" for a piece your aunt left you in 1998.

Frequently Asked

The eight questions every customer asks first.

If yours isn't here, call (503) 555-0142 or text the same number. Yolanda picks up.

How far in advance should I book my move?+
For local moves we ask for two to three weeks notice. For long-distance, six to eight weeks is ideal, especially May through August when crews fill up first. Last-minute moves are sometimes possible — we keep one truck reserved each weekend specifically for emergencies. Call and ask.
Do you give flat-rate quotes or hourly?+
Both. Local moves under thirty miles default to hourly at $89/hr per two-mover crew. Long-distance and white-glove moves are quoted flat-rate after a free in-home or video walkthrough. If you want a flat-rate on a local move, we can quote that too — usually slightly higher than hourly to account for the risk we're taking on.
Are you actually licensed and insured?+
Yes. USDOT 3214567, MC 1098765. We carry $1M general liability and full cargo coverage. Certificate of insurance is sent to your building manager on request, free of charge, usually within two hours of you asking. You can verify both license numbers at safer.fmcsa.dot.gov.
What does NOT come standard with a quote?+
Packing materials beyond what's needed for furniture protection (boxes, paper, tape for your kitchen and closets are an add-on), third-party piano riggers for pianos above 500 lbs that need crane access, and storage between move-out and move-in dates. We will price each of these as a separate line item before you sign anything.
Can you move a piano, gun safe or pool table?+
Yes. Marco runs our specialty rigging team. We handle upright and baby grand pianos up to 700 lbs, gun safes up to 1,200 lbs, and slate-bed pool tables disassembled and re-leveled in the new home. Anything heavier than that we sub to a certified rigger and quote the cost transparently — no markup.
What happens if something gets damaged?+
You file the claim through our office, not a third party. Yolanda responds within 48 hours, sends the same crew lead out to inspect the item, and we either pay you or have it professionally repaired within 30 days. We are members of AMSA, so if we somehow can't agree on the resolution, there's a binding dispute path. This has happened twice in eighteen years.
Do your crews work weekends and holidays?+
Saturdays yes, with no upcharge. Sundays by request only, and our crews are paid time-and-a-half on Sundays which is reflected in the quote. We don't book Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, or New Year's Day under any circumstances — those days are for our families and yours.
How do you screen the people who come into my home?+
Every crew member is a W-2 employee (no day labor, no contractors, no third-party staffing). Each is background checked at hire through a federal database, drug tested at hire and randomly thereafter, and goes through our six-week in-house training before they ride solo. The lead on every job has a minimum three years tenure with us. Names and photos of your specific crew arrive by text 24 hours before move day.
