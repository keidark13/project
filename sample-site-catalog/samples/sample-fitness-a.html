Why most people quit gyms by February

You're not lazy. You picked the wrong room.

Most gyms aren't built for the person who's nervous to walk in the door. They're built for the person who's already comfortable. We've spent ten years building the opposite of that.

The big-box intimidation problem

Rows of strangers on machines. No one will help you. The vibe says "if you don't know what you're doing, you don't belong." We don't run that room.

The hidden-fees trap

Joining fee. Maintenance fee. Cancellation fee. Locker fee. Annual rate hike. By month four you're paying more than you signed up for, and you can't get out without writing a letter.

Machines, mirrors, and zero community

You show up. You leave. Nobody learned your name. That's not a workout habit, that's a chore. People stick with gyms they actually like the people at.

Questions we get a lot

Common questions, honest answers.

If yours isn't here, the front desk picks up the phone. (555) 014-2200.

Do I have to sign a contract?
No. Every membership is month-to-month. You can cancel anytime in the member app with no fees and no notice period. We've never used a contract in our ten years — and we never will.
I haven't worked out in years. Will I feel out of place?
Most of our members started exactly where you are. Every class is scaled to your level by a coach who'll watch your form and step in if you're using too much weight or moving too fast. You will never be the only beginner in the room.
Can I use the equipment outside of class times?
Yes. All monthly and annual members get open-gym access during staffed hours — 5:30 AM to 9:30 PM weekdays and 7 AM to 6 PM weekends. A coach is on the floor the entire time if you want a form check.
What are your staffed hours?
Weekdays 5:30 AM to 9:30 PM. Weekends 7 AM to 6 PM. We're closed Christmas Day and Thanksgiving. Every other holiday runs a modified schedule that we post a week in advance.
Is parking available?
Yes. We have a free 24-space lot on Linden Avenue and overflow street parking on Belmont. Bike racks are at the front entrance. The 14 bus stops one block away on Hawthorne.
Are showers and lockers included?
Yes, at no extra cost. Full locker rooms with showers, towel service, hair dryers, and day-use lockers are included in every membership. Long-term lockers are $15/month if you want one of your own.
Do you offer childcare or kids programs?
We offer Saturday morning kids classes for ages 8 to 14 (Kids Strong, 10 AM). We do not currently offer drop-in childcare during adult sessions. Many of our parent members coordinate informal kid swaps between class times — we're happy to introduce you.
Can you help with nutrition or dietary questions?
Yes. Annual members get two free consultations with our in-house registered dietitian, Maria Velasquez (RD, CSSD). Monthly members can add on individual sessions at $65 each. We don't push supplements or meal-replacement products.
