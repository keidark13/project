What 45 minutes looks like

Five blocks.
Engineered transitions.

Every block has a purpose. The warm primes the nervous system. The intervals build the engine. The strength block protects the joints. The finisher empties the tank. The cool brings you back to room temperature without crashing.

00 — 05 MIN
Warm
Dynamic mobility flow. Hip openers, thoracic rotation, leg swings, arm circles. Pulse climbs from 70 to 110 BPM. Coach calls form cues. Doors close at 05:00.
05 — 25 MIN
Intervals
Four rounds of 4-minute work blocks. Rower, ski-erg, assault bike, kettlebell. Hit Z4 for 30-second peaks. Twenty seconds of recovery. Screens show your live BPM and zone.
25 — 35 MIN
Strength
Compound block: dumbbell goblet squat, single-arm row, push-press, deadlift. Three sets, 90-second rest. Heart-rate drops to Z2 — recovery is the work. Form over load. Coach corrects on the floor.
35 — 42 MIN
Finisher
Seven-minute EMOM or AMRAP. Burpees, kettlebell swings, slam balls. Empty the tank. This is where the cleanest 22 percent of your class time lives. Z4 the whole way.
42 — 45 MIN
Cool
Static stretch and breath protocol. Diaphragmatic 4-7-8. Pulse drops back to Z1. You walk out at room temperature. Recovery starts in the studio, not the locker room.

Frequently asked

Questions,
answered straight.

01Is a heart-rate monitor included?

Yes. Every member is fitted with a chest-strap monitor at check-in. Your live zone, BPM, and calories display on the studio screens during class and sync to your member dashboard after. Polar H10 quality, sanitized between every use.

02I've never done HIIT. Is this beginner-friendly?

Yes. Every interval has three tiered options on the board — foundation, push, and peak. Coaches scale you on the floor in your first three classes and your zones recalibrate from your live data. Roughly 30% of members are in their first ninety days. Nobody is staring at you. Everyone started here.

03Should I eat before class?

A light snack 60-90 minutes before class works for most people — banana, oatmeal, or toast with nut butter. Avoid heavy meals within an hour. Hydrate steadily through the day, not just at the door. If you're a 6:00 AM regular, fasted is fine once you've been at it three weeks.

04How large are classes?

Class capacity is 22 floor spots. Two coaches lead every class — one on the mic, one walking corrections. Booking opens 7 days out for drop-ins and 14 days out for unlimited. Most peak-time classes (6:00 AM, 5:30 PM, 6:30 PM) hit capacity 36-48 hours before. Mid-day and 8:00 PM tend to have same-day availability.

05What's the late policy?

Doors close at minute 5 for safety — the dynamic warm-up sequences your nervous system for the intervals. Arrive late and you'll be redirected to the next available class with no late charge once per cycle. Repeated late arrivals trigger a 24-hour cancellation hold. We don't take this stuff lightly because the protocol doesn't work backwards.

06Are showers and amenities provided?

All three locations have full showers, lockers (combination — no key required), towel service, Aesop products, blow dryers, and flat irons. Bring your own gym clothes and sneakers — we don't rent kit. Filtered water station at the door. Eucalyptus steam available at SoHo and Upper West post-class for an additional fee.

07Is there parking?

All three studios are designed around transit. SoHo (Lafayette) is a 4-minute walk from Bleecker Street on the 6. Williamsburg is two blocks from Bedford L. Upper West is one block from 72nd Street on the 1/2/3. Validated SpotHero rates are available for the West 73rd location. Citi Bike racks at all three doors.

08Can I pause my unlimited membership?

Yes. Unlimited members get one free 7-day pause per quarter and unlimited travel holds (3+ days, advance notice). No annual contract. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice — no fees, no exit interview, no upsell call. If you're moving out of NYC we'll waive the notice period. We've done this a few times.
