§ 02 — On restraint

Why twelve, and not two hundred.

The modern perfumery house produces, on average, eleven new compositions per year. We have produced twelve in twenty-six. The arithmetic is the manifesto: we believe a fragrance is closer to a building than to a season. It does not improve when hurried, and it does not survive the discount rack.

The library at Voile was not designed as a catalogue. It was designed as a vocabulary. Each fragrance is a complete sentence in that vocabulary; the three ranges — perfume, body, candle — are the three voices in which that sentence can be spoken. We do not blend body milks that match the perfume. We compose, separately and patiently, the body's own quieter answer.

Restraint, for us, is not asceticism. It is the courage to leave the gardenia out, to let the cedar sit unaccompanied for a full half-hour on skin, to print a dossier on uncoated stock and to ship it inside a recyclable box that does not pretend to be anything other than what it is.

This is a quieter house than the perfumery counters of the rue Saint-Honoré. It will remain a quieter house. We have no plans to grow Volume II beyond the twelve compositions currently named — and every plan to keep their quality at the standard at which they were first met.

Range No. 03 — Cire

Candle,

Four candles in beeswax and rice-bran wax. A single hand-spun cotton wick. Forty hours of burn.

§ 05 — A feature · Brume No. 04

Brume No. 04 —
the iris composition.

Forty-seven months. Three apprenticeship trips to Grasse. A composition that almost did not survive the second autumn.

Brume No. 04 began as a refusal. Camille Voile had written, in 2022, a brief that her then-mentor at Roure had quietly returned: iris root will not carry the smoke; the smoke will not carry the iris; you are asking the wrong question. The composition was shelved for fourteen months.

It returned in 2023 as a different question. Not can iris root carry smoke, but can the dry tobacco hold them apart long enough to listen to one another. The answer arrived in the third autumn, on the fifty-second variation: a composition in which the iris speaks for the first three hours, the tobacco for the second three, and the cedar — present from the first minute — closes the room six hours later.

Brume No. 04 is the one Camille keeps on her own dresser. It is also, by some distance, the one our stockists reorder most.

§ 07 — The makers

Two people, quietly
and at length.

Founding perfumer

Camille Voile

Composition · Brume · Lait

Camille founded the house in the autumn of 2020, after eighteen years inside the composition studios of Givaudan and a four-year apprenticeship under Jean-Claude Ellena. She wrote the founding brief for Brume No. 01 on the back of a Métro ticket during a strike, and she still keeps the ticket pressed into the first page of the house ledger.

She composes alone, by hand, on uncoated stock. The studio she works in is the same one in which she trained — six metres long, two windows, a single workbench made by her grandfather in 1948.

Co-founder · Cire

Augustine Lévy

Composition · Cire · Atelier programme

Augustine joined Camille in 2023, having spent fourteen years building the wax and candle programme at a Provençal estate. The four Cire compositions are his alone; the atelier visiting programme — and the relationships with each of the eight stockists — are also his.

He composes more quickly than Camille and is the house's reluctant administrator. He keeps the ledger, the supplier contacts, and the eighty-page document we send to every new stockist.

§ 09 — Frequently asked

Eight honest answers.

For more particular questions about composition, atelier visits, or stockist enquiries, write to us directly. We answer every letter, in order, within two working days.

01

How long does a Voile fragrance last on skin?

Six to nine hours, depending on individual skin chemistry, the time of day, and the season. Voile concentrations sit between eau de parfum and extrait: long enough to last a working day, short enough to remain personal — never a public announcement.

02

Can Voile fragrances be layered?

Yes — and the library is composed for it. The three ranges share three crossing accords: cedar (Brume), neroli (Lait), vetiver (Cire). Any two compositions within a single range will share at least one structural note. Across ranges, we recommend pairing a Lait composition under a Brume — never two Brume compositions at once.

03

Which fragrance families are represented?

Woody, floral leather, hesperidic, smoke, and a single solifore (Brume No. 04, iris). We do not produce gourmand or aquatic compositions; nor do we currently produce a chypre, though one is in composition for Volume III.

04

Are the compositions suitable for sensitive skin?

All twelve compositions are IFRA Category 4 compliant. Full allergen disclosure — twenty-six declared materials under the EU Cosmetics Regulation — is printed on every bottle and listed in the dossier shipped with each order. Patch testing is recommended for any new fragrance; samples make this straightforward.

05

Do you offer samples?

Yes. The library may be sampled in 5ml decants — twenty applications, sufficient to wear a composition for several days and through more than one season. A discovery set of three decants is $48, with the full amount credited toward the purchase of any full bottle.

06

How is a fragrance gifted?

Each bottle ships in a recyclable archival box with a hand-signed dossier, a single sheet of cotton tissue, and a slim envelope for a written note. Personal inscription on the dossier is available at the Paris atelier by appointment; gift cards are available in any denomination.

07

Do you offer wholesale?

Voile maintains relationships with eight stockists across four continents. New stockist applications are reviewed twice per year, in March and September, by Camille and Augustine personally. We do not work with distributors or department-store conglomerates; we do not appear on third-party marketplaces.

08

How do I visit the atelier?

The Paris atelier — 14 rue des Archives, in the 4ème arrondissement — receives visitors by appointment only, Tuesday through Friday. A consultation runs ninety minutes, includes a walk-through of the full library, and is offered without charge. Write to atelier@voile.example to request a date.
