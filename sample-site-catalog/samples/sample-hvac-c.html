03 What makes us different

Three commitments that separate a studio from a service company.

01

Design before equipment

Every project begins with a Manual J load calc, a Manual D duct design, and a walkthrough with the owner. We size for your actual house — not a rule-of-thumb based on square footage. Most homes we touch had the wrong tonnage before we arrived.

02

Inverter-first, always

We install Daikin Fit and Carrier Greenspeed inverter systems exclusively. Quieter, more efficient at part-load, and they don’t cycle on-and-off like a window unit. Your home maintains temperature within 0.5°F instead of swinging 4°F.

03

One dedicated service manager

You don’t call a dispatch queue. You call Daniel, or you call Priya. They know your system, your home, your last visit, and your preference for morning appointments. Same person picks up year five as year one.

08 Questions

Things owners ask before booking.

Do you handle repairs, or only full system installs?

Both, though our heart is in design-and-install for renovation and new-construction projects. For existing Atelier-installed systems, service is included for Stewardship members and billed at $185/hour for non-members. We don’t take service calls on systems we didn’t install — the diagnostic time on unknown installations isn’t fair to either of us.

What does a typical project cost?

Our average residential install (3,000-4,500 sq ft, full system replacement with some ductwork modification) runs $24,000 to $58,000. New construction and major retrofits with full duct redesign run higher. Every project starts with a $1,200 design phase, which is credited toward the install if you proceed. No surprises, full quote in writing before any equipment is ordered.

How long does the consultation-to-completion process take?

Typically 6-12 weeks from first consultation to commissioning. Two weeks for design, two weeks for owner review and equipment lead time, then 4-21 days on-site depending on scope. We don’t do emergency replacements — if your system has died and you need cooling tomorrow, we’ll refer you to one of two trusted service partners.

Why don’t you publish prices on the website?

Because every house is different, and a price without a load calc is fiction. The contractors who quote $7,499 over the phone are the same ones who deliver a unit that cycles every six minutes and shaves three years off its life. The $1,200 design fee is the price of an honest answer.

Are you really inverter-only? What if I want a traditional single-stage system?

We respectfully decline. Single-stage AC systems are the last decade’s technology — they cycle, they’re noisy, they can’t dehumidify well at part-load, and they wear out faster. We’d rather not install something we wouldn’t put in our own homes.

What credentials does your team hold?

All our installers are NATE-certified (Heat Pump and AC Service specializations), EPA 608 Universal, and OSHA-30. Our design lead is a licensed mechanical engineer (P.E.). We’re a Daikin Premier Partner and a Carrier Factory Authorized Dealer. State contractor license #HVAC-148229, fully bonded and insured.
