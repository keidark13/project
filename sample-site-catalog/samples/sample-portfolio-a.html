04 — Process

How a typical engagement runs.

Four stages, each with a defined deliverable. Most engagements move through this in four to twelve weeks.

01

Discover

Two days of audit reading, stakeholder interviews, and product walkthroughs. Output is a single document with the shipping plan.

02

Sketch

Low-fidelity exploration, motion comps in Figma or Origami, and a tight loop with the team on critical screens.

03

Build

Production code in your repo. PRs sized for review. Pairing with your engineers wherever the work crosses into your existing system.

04

Ship

Rollout plan, internal docs, and a 30-day support window. Most engagements end with one written retrospective.

07 — About

A short bio.

NP

I am Naomi Park, a design engineer based in Brooklyn. I run a one-person studio that takes on a handful of product engagements each year. Most of the work lands in the intersection between design systems, motion engineering, and frontend infrastructure.

Before going independent I worked in-house at Stripe on Checkout, at Vercel on the marketing site, and at Linear on the early component library. The studio opened in 2024 and has since shipped engagements with teams in New York, Berlin, San Francisco, and Tokyo.

I write a quiet journal of field notes on motion, system design, and the small rituals of shipping work. Occasionally I publish in long-form journals. I read more print than feed and prefer black coffee, calm rooms, and one tab at a time.
