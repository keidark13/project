How are you different from a typical agent at a big brokerage?

Big-brokerage agents run a volume model - more clients, more listings, faster cycle. We cap intake at 22 active engagements per year. That's why we can pre-walk every home, draft same-day offers, and stay involved 12 months past closing.

Do you cover both buying and selling?

Yes. About 60% of our work is buyer-side, 40% sell-side. Many clients hire us for both - list with us, buy through us, one transition.

What price range do you typically work in?

$450K to $4.2M residential. Sweet spot is $700K-$1.6M. We don't take investment-property-only briefs (different muscle, different relationships).

What does "off-market" actually mean?

Listings we know about through our broker-to-broker network 7-30 days before they hit MLS. About 30-40% of our buyer closings are off-market sourced.

What's the brief intake call?

25 minutes by phone or in person. You describe what you're looking for, we ask the questions you've never been asked, and at the end either we agree to work together or we refer you out. No pitch, no sign-up.
