FAQ

Eight questions, eight straight answers.

The ones our dispatchers field most. Anything not covered here: call 1-855-768-8947 or chat in the app.

How accurate is the 60-second quote?
The figure you see is a flat rate — not an estimate. As long as the inventory you select matches what we load on move day, you pay exactly what the app showed. If your home has heavy or specialty items we did not see, our dispatcher confirms any change with you before the truck rolls.
Can you really move me the same day?
In most service metros, yes. Book before 10:00 a.m. local time for a same-day window. Bookings after that get next-morning service. The app shows live ETA based on crew availability in your ZIP code, so you can see before you lock whether a same-day slot is realistic.
Do I have to pay a deposit?
No. We don't run a card until the crew arrives at your door. You confirm the inventory, the crew lead authorizes the flat rate, and the charge posts after delivery is signed. If you're moving from out of state, we may verify the card but never authorize against it pre-move.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
Cancel or reschedule free from the app up to 60 seconds before the crew is dispatched. Once a truck is en route, a small rolling fee applies — shown live in your booking screen so there are no surprises. The reschedule flow auto-suggests the next two open slots in your area.
Are the crews employees or contractors?
RouteWise crews are W-2 employees of our partner carriers. Every team lead is background-checked, drug-screened, and has at least 18 months of moving experience. We don't use day-labor and we don't allow sub-subcontracting — the names on your crew sheet are the names that show up.
How much insurance is included?
Every move includes $1,000,000 bonded liability coverage plus $0.60 per pound released-value protection at no charge. Full-value protection is available at checkout for $89 (Solo), $129 (Standard), or $189 (Premium). High-value declarations for art, instruments, or jewelry can be added per item in the inventory step.
Do you handle long-distance moves?
Yes — we cover the lower 48 states. Long-distance quotes still take 60 seconds; the app uses live carrier capacity to lock a guaranteed delivery window of three to seven days depending on lane. We provide a verified weight ticket attached to every long-distance invoice so the price is impossible to game.
What about pianos, safes, or oversized items?
Add specialty items in the inventory step — pianos (upright, baby grand, grand), safes up to 1,200 lb, hot tubs, and pool tables are all flat-rate. We dispatch a piano-rated team automatically when those items are present. The app flags whether a stair-walker, hoist, or window rig is required and adjusts the crew accordingly.
