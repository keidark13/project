FAQ

The questions every founder asks on call one.

Is this group coaching or 1:1?
1:1 only. There is no group cohort, no group calls, and no shared community. The peer bench introductions are direct, not a Slack.
What revenue range do you work with?
$500K to roughly $3M ARR. Below that, the constraint is usually offer or acquisition, which is a different specialty. Above $3M, you usually need an operator hire, not a coach.
How much time does this take from me per week?
Two 90-minute calls per month. Voxer is asynchronous - reply when you can. Most clients spend 3-4 hours total on the work per week, including implementation.
Do you guarantee a revenue outcome?
No. Anyone who guarantees a specific revenue number in coaching is lying to you. We guarantee the work, the framework, and the 30-day exit if it isn't a fit.
What if I'm not actually stuck - I just want to scale faster?
Then this probably isn't the right fit. The Operator Intensive is built for founders hitting a ceiling. If the business is already compounding, your bottleneck is somewhere else.
