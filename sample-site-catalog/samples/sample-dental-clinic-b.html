FAQ

Questions we hear in the first call.

Will the fixed-fee quote really not change?+

Yes, and we put it in writing before you book a placement. The only situation that changes the price is one you'd approve in advance: adding a second site to the same surgical visit, for example. Standard add-ons that other clinics surprise-bill (bone graft, sedation, follow-up adjustments) are already inside the package.

Do you accept my insurance?+

We're in-network with Delta Dental and Aetna PPO, and we file out-of-network claims for most other major carriers. The $250 diagnostic includes a full pre-authorization report so you see your actual out-of-pocket cost before any work starts.

How is a single-visit crown actually possible?+

Two pieces of equipment most clinics don't keep in-house: a high-resolution intraoral scanner and a chairside CAD/CAM mill. We capture the prep, design the restoration on screen, and the mill carves it from a ceramic block in about 15-20 minutes while you wait.

What if I need a procedure you don't offer in-house?+

For the small set of cases that need orthognathic surgery or full-mouth reconstruction beyond our scope, we coordinate with two vetted specialists and stay the lead on your case so you're not stranded between offices.

How long does a typical implant placement appointment take?+

Single-site implant with same-day crown: 90-120 minutes from numbing to final fit. Multi-site cases are planned across two sessions, scheduled in the same week.

Do you offer payment plans?+

Yes. We work with CareCredit and Sunbit for 0% APR plans on 6, 12, and 18-month terms for cases over $1,200. The diagnostic visit covers all the paperwork.
