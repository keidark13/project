Frequently asked

The stuff people Google before they book.

How does the 30-minute on-time guarantee actually work?

When you book, you get a 60-minute arrival window. If the tech hasn’t knocked on the door by the end of that window plus 30 minutes, $100 is automatically credited to your invoice. You don’t need to ask, complain, or file anything — the dispatch system flags it and our office applies the credit before the tech leaves. We paid out 41 of these credits last year. We’d rather pay them than break the promise.

What does the $89 diagnostic cover?

$89 covers the trip out, the 60-minute window, and a full systems check on whichever piece of equipment is acting up — HVAC, plumbing, or electrical. You get a written flat-rate quote before any repair work begins. If you approve the quote, the $89 credits against the repair total (so the diagnostic is effectively free).

Do you really cover all three trades with one company?

Yes. Every Bluecrew tech holds at least two trade licenses, most hold three. Our 11 senior techs are dual-master (HVAC + plumbing or HVAC + electrical). It means one truck, one invoice, one warranty — and no “not my department” when the leak under the water heater turns out to be a wiring issue.

Are you really available 24/7? Including holidays?

Yes — 365 days a year, including Christmas. We staff our after-hours line with our own techs on rotating on-call, not a national call center. After-hours service has a $149 trip charge (also credited if you book the repair).

What financing do you offer?

0% APR for 18 months on jobs over $4,000 (Wells Fargo or Synchrony, approval in 5 minutes). Longer terms with low APR available on full system replacements over $10K.

How are your techs screened?

Federal background check + 5-panel drug screen before the first ride-along, re-screened annually. You get a photo + bio of the assigned tech via text 60 minutes before they arrive.
