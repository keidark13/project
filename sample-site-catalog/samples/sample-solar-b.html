Do I really need a battery, or just panels?

Panels alone cut your bill but go dark with the grid. A battery is what gives you true independence: stored power for night use and backup when the grid fails. We size both together so you only pay for the backup you actually need.

What happens during an outage?

Your battery automatically takes over and keeps your essential circuits running, often for a full day or more depending on the load. When the sun returns, the panels recharge it. Most homeowners barely notice the grid went down.

How much does going solar plus storage cost?

It depends on your roof, usage, and how much backup you want, which is exactly what the free plan measures. Many homeowners pay $0 down through financing and replace their utility bill with a lower fixed payment.

Do I really get the tax credit?

If you owe federal tax and own the system, you typically qualify, and battery storage is included. We break down your exact eligibility in writing during the plan, never a vague promise.

What if I sell my house?

Owned solar plus storage generally adds resale value and transfers with the home. We walk you through it so there are no surprises.
