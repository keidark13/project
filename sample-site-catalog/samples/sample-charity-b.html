The first-gen tax

No one tells you the cost of going first.

When you are the first in your family to apply to college, every form is a stranger, every cost is a surprise, and every doubt arrives at midnight without a translator. These are the three weights our scholars carry on day one.

The application maze

Common App. FAFSA. CSS Profile. Recommendation letters. Essay portals. Twelve different deadlines, six different logins, and no one at the kitchen table who has done this before.

Most first-gen students apply to half as many schools as their peers and apply to schools that admit them at half the rate. Not because they are not ready. Because no one mapped the maze for them.

The financial aid mystery

The sticker price is $68,000. The actual price is somewhere between $4,000 and $40,000. You will only know the real number after you commit. Until then, you are asked to make the largest financial decision of your life from inside a fog.

Light Bridge sits with families through every aid letter, runs the net-price calculations, and finds the dollars that schools quietly leave on the table.

The imposter feeling

You sit in your first lecture and realize half the room already knows the professor's first book. You learn to write essays in a register your parents do not speak. You learn that some questions are not safe to ask out loud.

A mentor who has felt that same chair, in that same room, is the cheapest and rarest form of belonging we have ever found. We give every student one.

The Light Bridge Way

A four-year hand on the shoulder.

Most college access programs end at orientation. We start there. Match, mentor, fund, graduate — the four steps every scholar walks with us.

Step 01

Match

We pair every scholar with a mentor who matches their field, geography, and first-gen story. 218 active mentors, average 12-year career.

Step 02

Mentor

One hour a week, 24 months. Calls, texts, the late-night essay panic, the first interview prep. Real humans, not a curriculum.

Step 03

Fund

We co-fund scholarships with 41 partner universities, cover application fees, and quietly carry the gaps the aid letter missed.

Step 04

Graduate

89% of our scholars enroll in college. 76% graduate on time. Then they come back as mentors. The bridge only works if it stays standing.

A scholar's story

Maya, in her own words.

Maya grew up in a two-bedroom apartment on Cherokee Street, the oldest of three. Her mother cleaned offices on the overnight shift, came home at 6 a.m., and slept until Maya left for school. They did not talk about college the way other families did. They talked about whether the rent would clear.

A high school counselor sent her name to Light Bridge in her junior year. Two weeks later she was matched with Alana, a corporate attorney who had grown up in Cape Girardeau and was the first in her family to finish a four-year degree. They started with a phone call. They ended up writing seventeen drafts of one personal essay.

My mom worked three shifts so I could study one. Alana taught me the third was mine to spend, not to repay. Maya R., Class of 2025

Maya was accepted to nine universities and chose Washington University on a Light Bridge co-funded scholarship. She graduated last May with a degree in public health and a job offer from a community clinic in North Saint Louis. Her two younger siblings are now in our program. Her mother came to the graduation in a borrowed dress and cried for the second time in twenty years.

What we do

Six programs. One bridge.

Every scholar moves through every program. We do not pick favorites. We pick the cohort and then we show up for all of them, every year, until the diploma is in their hand.

1:1 mentorship

Two-year minimum pairing with a vetted adult mentor. Weekly hour. Field-matched, story-matched, location-flexible.

Application coaching

Common App, FAFSA, CSS Profile, school-specific essays. Twelve coaching sessions per junior, eight per senior, all free.

Scholarship matching

We co-fund scholarships with 41 partner universities and surface a database of 600+ private awards our scholars qualify for.

Summer bridge program

Three-week residential in August before freshman year. Writing, study skills, campus navigation, room and board fully covered.

Family workshops

Quarterly evening sessions for parents and guardians. Translated into Spanish and Vietnamese. Childcare and dinner included.

Alumni network

Graduates become mentors, recruiters, and donors. 142 alumni currently active. The bridge stays open in both directions.

From the founder

I was the first in my family too. I know what it costs to find the door alone.

I grew up in a small town in eastern Texas, the daughter of a hospital janitor and a seamstress. I applied to college on a public library computer. My acceptance letter came addressed to my mother because I had used her name on the form by mistake, and I will never forget her reading it to me out loud, slowly, like she was double-checking that the words were really there.

I made it through college, and then graduate school, and then a career in public policy. Along the way I noticed something: every time I succeeded, there had been a single adult in the room who had known the answer to a question I did not know how to ask. That person was never my parents. They could not have been. They had given me everything they had, and the thing I still needed was a map.

Light Bridge exists because that map shouldn't be luck. Every first-generation student deserves an adult in the room who has stood in the same hallway, signed the same forms, and felt the same fear. We have built a program where that adult always shows up, on time, for four years, free of charge.

If you sponsor a scholar with us, you are not buying tuition. You are buying a hand on the shoulder at 11 p.m. the night before the FAFSA closes. That hand is the entire reason Light Bridge exists. Thank you for considering being it.

— Dr. Lena Ortiz

Honest answers

Questions we hear most.

How are students chosen for Light Bridge?
Students are nominated by school counselors, community partners, and through self-referral. We prioritize first-generation applicants with a household income under 200% of the federal poverty line. Our admissions committee meets quarterly and reviews around 1,400 applicants per cycle. We accept roughly 18% of nominees, prioritizing fit with available mentors and capacity in the scholar's region.
Where does my donation actually go?
82 cents of every dollar goes directly to programs: mentor stipends, scholarship co-funding, application fees, and summer bridge housing. 11 cents covers admin (payroll, rent, insurance) and 7 cents covers fundraising. Our 990 is published openly every May and our audited financials are linked on the footer of this page.
Can I mentor instead of donating money?
Yes, and we need mentors as much as we need donors. Mentors commit to one hour per week for 24 months. We run a 6-hour onboarding, a full background check, and pair you based on field of study, geography, and lived experience. We currently have 218 active mentors and a waitlist of 47 students. Apply to mentor via the mentor form linked in the footer.
Do you serve students outside of Missouri?
Yes. We started in Saint Louis in 2014 and now serve students in 22 states. The mentorship runs virtually, the application coaching is national, and we partner with 41 universities across the country for matched scholarship dollars. The summer bridge program is residential in Saint Louis, with travel covered for any out-of-state scholar.
Are donations tax-deductible?
Yes. Light Bridge is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 47-2841093). Donations are tax-deductible to the full extent permitted by US law. You will receive an immediate emailed receipt for any one-time gift, and an annual giving statement mailed in January for all gifts in the previous calendar year.
How long are students supported?
Four academic years, plus a senior-year transition program into employment or graduate school. After graduation, alumni move into our peer-mentor network, and many return to mentor and coach the next cohort. The longest active relationship in our program is now eleven years old, and we are proud of that.
What if my company wants to partner?
We offer corporate sponsorship at the cohort level ($25,000 funds a full cohort year of 14 students) and matched-giving programs for employees. Most of our corporate partners also host summer internships for our rising juniors and seniors. We have current partnerships with 17 companies across finance, technology, healthcare, and law. Contact partnerships@lightbridge.org to explore a fit.
Can I sponsor a specific student?
Sponsors at the $1,000/month Co-Scholarship tier are matched with a named scholar, receive a quarterly note from them, and are invited to the annual in-person Saint Louis donor dinner. Lower tiers are pooled across the full cohort. Regardless of tier, every donor receives quarterly impact reports with stories, enrollment data, and financials.

Will you sponsor Maya's next chapter?

We have 47 students on our waiting list right now. Each one of them has the grades, the grit, and the dream. What they don't have is the single adult in the room. That is the gift you give.
