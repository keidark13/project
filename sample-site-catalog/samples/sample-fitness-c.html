A note from the founders

Why wellness fragmented into twelve apps.

— A. Hayes & J. Okafor
Founders, Ellington House

The wellness industry sold us subscriptions before it sold us coherence. A gym at $200 a month. A boutique yoga studio at $80. A nutrition app at $50. A sauna pass at $90. A sleep tracker, a hydration tracker, a continuous glucose monitor, an IV clinic two neighborhoods away. The bill arrives at six hundred dollars and what you have purchased, mostly, is logistics.

Each vendor optimizes a single variable in isolation. The strength coach does not know your sleep score. The dietitian does not know your recovery load. The IV nurse has never spoken to your trainer. The data lives in twelve places and the synthesis happens nowhere — or, more accurately, it happens on your phone, at midnight, by you, with a spreadsheet you will not maintain.

Ellington House was built on the premise that wellness is not a stack of subscriptions but a single discipline, practiced under one roof, by practitioners who speak to each other on Monday morning. The strength lead writes notes the recovery lead reads. The dietitian sees your bloodwork. The sleep coach knows your training schedule. Coherence is the product.

We are not the cheapest place to work out in Manhattan. We are not trying to be. We are trying to be the only place you need.

The Ellington Model

Six disciplines, one membership, one calendar.

Each service is led by a senior practitioner with clinical or competitive credentials. There is no retail floor, no commissioned upsell, no per-class billing.

01

Strength & movement

Two private floors per location with German engineered platforms, a full free-weight room, and dedicated mobility space. Programming is built around an individual annual plan, not a class schedule.

  • Quarterly performance assessment
  • 1:1 sessions twice weekly included at Founder tier
  • Open-floor coaching all operating hours
02

Recovery suite

Contrast therapy as a daily practice, not a quarterly indulgence. Infrared sauna, traditional Finnish, cold plunge at 38°F, and a quiet recovery lounge with circadian lighting.

  • Unlimited access during operating hours
  • Linen and robe service included
  • Recovery-coach-led contrast protocols weekly
03

Registered dietitian

Quarterly consultations with credentialed dietitians who read your bloodwork, train alongside our strength leads, and treat nutrition as an applied science — not a content category.

  • Bloodwork review and protocol design
  • Performance and longevity programming
  • No supplement commissions, ever
04

Cryotherapy & sauna

Whole-body cryotherapy chambers at −166°F maintained on a clinical schedule. Used in conjunction with sauna and plunge to manage systemic inflammation across training cycles.

  • Three-minute chamber sessions
  • Protocol-paired with training week
  • Supervised by trained technicians
05

IV therapy

A discreet, clinically supervised lounge with rotating protocols developed by our medical advisor. Hydration, NAD+, glutathione, methylated B-complex, and recovery-specific formulations.

  • Licensed nurses on staff
  • Medical advisor protocol-review
  • Available by appointment, weekdays
06

Sleep coaching

A dedicated coach reviews ring or watch data fortnightly, runs structured wind-down protocols, and works alongside the dietitian when sleep failure is metabolic rather than behavioral.

  • Bi-weekly data review
  • Light, temperature, and timing protocols
  • Travel-zone re-entrainment plans

M. K. arrived at Ellington House the way most members arrive — by accounting. A spreadsheet, late one Sunday, totalling the year’s spend across a gym, a yoga studio, a nutritionist on the Upper East Side, a sauna pass downtown, and a quarterly bloodwork subscription. The number was uncomfortable. The coordination was worse.

The first six months were structural. A baseline assessment with the head of strength, a panel review with the dietitian, a sleep audit that surfaced an undiagnosed apnea pattern, and a contrast-therapy schedule timed to a brutal travel calendar between New York, London, and Singapore. Nothing exotic. Just the same work, done by people who spoke to each other.

“I stopped thinking about wellness as a stack. I think about it as a room I walk into four mornings a week, where everyone already knows the file.” — M. K., venture partner, member since 2023

In year two the dietitian flagged a subclinical iron pattern the IV lounge then addressed under medical supervision. The sleep coach re-entrained M. K. across three transatlantic trips inside a month. The strength lead programmed around a partially repaired ACL without a single referral out. The compounding effect, over thirty-six months, is not visible in any single session. It is visible in the absence of injury and the presence of energy.

M. K. is not unusual at Ellington. The room skews professional, late-thirties to mid-fifties, with the kind of calendar that punishes incoherence. The bet they have made — and the bet the club has made on them — is that the body, treated as infrastructure rather than a hobby, repays the investment.

A reciprocal commitment

Five terms, quietly in your favour.

Fitness contracts are usually written as a one-way punishment. Ours are written as a relationship. Five terms make the relationship explicit.

  • i.
    30-day initial assessment The first month is structured as a mutual fit evaluation. If either side declines to continue, the initiation fee is refunded in full.
  • ii.
    Quarterly health-marker tracking Bloodwork, recovery, and performance markers reviewed by the dietitian and medical advisor — the data drives the programming, not the other way around.
  • iii.
    Member-locker amenity Permanent locker with linen and grooming amenities stocked between visits. Founders receive bespoke product preference at intake.
  • iv.
    Hold for travel Up to 90 days of paused billing per calendar year, with no penalty. Founders retain partner-club access in London and Los Angeles during hold.
  • v.
    No annual contract Month-to-month, always. We earn the renewal by being useful, not by writing punitive cancellation clauses.
Frequently asked

Questions worth asking before you join.

Eight answers we’ve made a habit of giving in full on the first tour. The rest happen in conversation.

Ask in person →
01 How does the membership process actually work? +
A private tour, a baseline assessment with the head of strength, a panel review with the dietitian, and a programming proposal. The first thirty days are a structured assessment. If we are not the right room for you, we say so — and refund the initiation fee in full.
02 Can I bring a guest, and how often? +
Member tier includes two guest day-passes per quarter. Founder tier extends unlimited weekday guest privileges and a permanent +1 on recovery services. Out-of-town guests of Founders are welcome on weekends with 24 hours’ notice.
03 Where are the three locations — and which is right for me? +
Crosby Street (SoHo, full clinical floor), Madison Avenue (Upper East Side, larger recovery suite), and West 15th Street (Meatpacking, largest strength floor and rooftop sauna). All members access all three. Most members nominate a home location at intake.
04 What are the operating hours? +
05:30 to 22:00, seven days. Recovery suites operate until 22:30. Founders may request after-hours private use of the strength floor or recovery suite with 48 hours’ notice, subject to availability.
05 Is towel and linen service included? +
Yes. Workout towels, robes, and bath linens are included at every visit. Members receive a permanent locker that holds personal items between visits, restocked daily by the housekeeping team.
06 Do you sell supplements or earn commissions? +
No retail floor. No commissioned recommendations. The dietitians maintain a curated, evidence-based formulary and will recommend specific products when warranted — but the club earns nothing on what is recommended. That is a deliberate structural choice.
07 How do dietary consultations and bloodwork reviews work? +
Members receive a quarterly registered-dietitian consultation. Bring your own bloodwork from any provider, or use our partner labs at member rates. Founders receive monthly consultations, plus quarterly review with the medical advisor on more complex protocols.
08 Can I hold my membership during long travel? +
Yes. Up to ninety days per calendar year, paused at no charge. Founder members retain access to our partner clubs in London (Mayfair) and Los Angeles (Venice) during travel holds, and may continue recovery and consultation services at either.
