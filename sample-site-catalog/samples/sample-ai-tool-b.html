FAQ

Questions creators always ask

What file formats does Glimmer support?
MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, and audio-only formats including MP3, WAV, and M4A. Direct imports from Zoom, Riverside, Descript, and Loom are supported via OAuth. We auto-detect frame rate, audio sample rate, and channel layout — nothing to configure on your end.
Is there a maximum recording length?
Creator plan supports recordings up to 2 hours per file. Studio plan supports up to 4 hours. Enterprise has no length cap and adds multi-camera multitrack uploads so you can ingest a full live show with separate guest tracks.
What export resolutions are available?
Up to 1080p on Creator, up to 4K on Studio and Enterprise. Vertical 9:16, square 1:1, and widescreen 16:9 are exported simultaneously from a single source — no separate renders required, no aspect-ratio fees.
Will AI processing cost extra on top of my plan?
No. Render minutes are included in every plan. Creator includes 300 minutes per month, Studio 1500, Enterprise unlimited. Overage rolls to next month for 30 days at no additional cost. We will email you at 80 percent of quota so nothing surprises you.
How accurate is the transcript?
Word error rate sits around 4 percent on clean studio audio and 7 percent on Zoom-quality audio. You can edit the transcript inline and the clip regenerates in seconds. We also keep a per-speaker vocabulary so industry terms learn over time.
Do you train models on my uploads?
No. Your media is processed in an isolated tenant, never used for training, and encrypted at rest with AES-256. On Enterprise you hold your own keys. SOC 2 Type II report available on request.
How long do you keep my source files?
Source files stay for 30 days on Creator, 90 days on Studio, and 365 days on Enterprise. You can trigger immediate deletion at any time from the dashboard and download a deletion receipt for your records.
Does Glimmer work for non-English content?
Yes. 38 languages are supported for transcription and highlight detection, including Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Japanese, Hindi, and Tagalog. Auto-translated captions across all 38 languages are included on Studio and above so a single record can ship globally.
