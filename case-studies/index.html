01

US Fragrance E-Commerce Store

16x ad spend scale in one month — while ROAS improved — on an account that started with counterfeit-policy disapprovals.

The problem

  • 8 products disapproved under Google's counterfeit policy, risky title patterns across the catalog
  • Product titles disconnected from what shoppers actually search
  • Brand-new ads account that had to scale without tripping more policy flags

What I did

  • Rewrote all 8 flagged listings policy-safe, plus title rules so new products can't repeat the violation
  • 5,718-keyword study filtered through honesty gates → 450-row keyword-led shopping feed covering 1.2M+ monthly searches
  • Negative-keyword banks and a branded/generic split so budget scaled into the right queries

02

Supplements Brand — 5 English-Speaking Markets

From a $0 cold start to a 2x ROAS month — after rescuing the bestseller from a drug-policy flag that made it invisible in Shopping.

The problem

  • Bestselling product line kept getting flagged under Google's drug-class policies — effectively invisible in Shopping
  • Cold-start ads account: no history, five markets at once (AU, CA, UK, IE, NZ)

What I did

  • Root-caused the flag: a drug-class term in copy, not the brand name — rebuilt listings brand-led and policy-clean
  • 1,539-row supplemental feed in two languages with search-grounded titles
  • Full API account diagnostic: zero-converting products, starved budgets, compliance timebombs → prioritized fix plan + negatives pack

Conversions285

July overall: $6,574 at ~2x blended ROAS while still scaling. Brand search at 7.86x. Merchant Center at 94.4% eligible products after the policy cleanup.

Source: Google Ads account (screenshots) + Google Ads API weekly pulls + Merchant Center product-status scan, Jul 24, 2026.

03

Canadian Fashion Boutique — 4,547-Variant Feed Rebuild

Every one of 4,547 product variants retitled around real search demand — 99.8% Merchant Center eligibility at launch.

The problem

  • 4,547-variant catalog running on template titles — zero connection to search demand
  • A niche where Google watches misrepresentation closely — sloppy titles get accounts suspended

What I did

  • Pulled 21,526 keywords with real volumes from the Google Ads API (Canada geo) — demand data, not guesswork
  • Exclusive-assignment system: 1,827 keyword heads, each owned by exactly one product — listings never compete for the same query
  • Every title through honesty gates: claims verified against the live site, competitor brands stripped, ungrounded copy killed
